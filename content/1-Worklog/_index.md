This worklog records the 12-week **First Cloud AI Journey** programme, running **11/05/2026 – 31/07/2026**.

The first three weeks were a self-paced sprint through the complete [AWS Cloud Journey](https://cloudjourney.awsstudygroup.com/) curriculum — all **296 videos** ([playlist](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLahN4TLWtox2a3vElknwzU_urND8hLn1i)) — building the theory before touching the console. Weeks 4 to 10 were hands-on lab weeks: one service group per week, each one producing a concrete artefact in the AWS Console that the capstone project would later reuse. The last two weeks covered ideation, the proposal and full execution of the **Toxic Text Moderation Platform**, ending with the report submission on 31/07/2026.

The HCMUT external internship (TTNT, HK253) is registered for **15/06/2026 – 14/08/2026**, the period stated in the internship programme that AWS Vietnam registered with the Faculty (Form D2). Weeks 1–5 of this worklog are the preparatory self-study phase completed before that window opens in Week 6; after the 31/07 submission the team continued through 14/08 in a feedback and system-enhancement phase, described in section 7.
