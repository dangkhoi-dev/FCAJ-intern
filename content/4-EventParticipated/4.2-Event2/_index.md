### FCAJ Community Day — June 2026 (livestream)

&emsp;**Date & time:** 27/06/2026

&emsp;**Location:** Online — live broadcast on the **AWS Study Group** YouTube channel

&emsp;**Role:** Attendee (followed the live broadcast)

&emsp;**Recording:** [FCAJ Community Day - June 2026](https://www.youtube.com/live/G8-WlI7f6dE?feature=share)

#### Event content

The June Community Day was run as a hybrid session: speakers presented to a room of program participants at the venue while the whole session was streamed live on the AWS Study Group channel, so members who could not attend in person could still follow along and ask questions in the chat. The format was the one the program uses for its community days — program updates, speaker sessions from the FCAJ community, and open Q&A between segments.

I followed this one through the livestream rather than in the room. The full recording remains available at the link above, which also means anything covered in the session can be re-watched instead of relying on notes — something I ended up doing more than once while writing this report.

#### Lessons and value gained

The clearest takeaway from this one was less about a specific service and more about how experienced speakers structure a technical story: the sessions that landed opened with the problem and the constraint before naming a single AWS service, and only then showed the architecture. When I later wrote the team blog post and the Proposal section of this report, I deliberately used that order — problem first, then why the existing options fall short, then the architecture — instead of leading with a service list.

Attending online also made a practical point that shaped how our team worked afterwards: a recorded session is a reusable asset in a way a live one is not. Knowing the stream would stay up let me watch it once for the overall picture and return to specific segments later, and it is the reason I now treat the AWS Study Group channel as a reference source rather than a one-off event feed.
